# Starting Semester Six

New fall semester at Sejong University and this one has a noticeably different shape than the last few — less introductory theory, more courses that assume you already know how to build things and now want you to build them well.

## What's on the schedule

- **Problem Solving and Lab: JAVA** — structured problem-solving with Java as the vehicle, likely the most hands-on course of the semester.
- **History of Engineering Technology** — a step back from implementation to look at how the field actually got here, which I think is easy to undervalue until you're the one making decisions that later engineers will have to live with.
- **Digital Signal Processing** — new territory for me, and the one I expect to be the steepest climb this semester.
- **Algorithms and Lab** — formalizing a lot of intuition I've built from independent projects into actual, provable complexity analysis and design patterns.
- **Advanced C Programming and Lab** — going deeper into a language I already use regularly, which usually means the gaps in what I *thought* I understood are about to get exposed.

## The goal for this semester

Straightforward: at least a 4.0/4.5 GPA this semester. My cumulative sits at 3.76/4.5 right now, and this is the semester to push it meaningfully upward rather than just maintain it.

## Why I'm writing this down

Mostly so there's a record to hold myself to. It's easy to set a GPA goal in week one and quietly let it slide by week ten. Publishing it here means there's an actual, dated commitment sitting on my own site — and a clear point of comparison when the semester's done.
